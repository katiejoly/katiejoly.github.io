ABOUT

Meet Katie

My work is rooted in women’s health, patient education, and the belief that exceptional care deserves equally thoughtful communication.

With more than thirteen years of experience in patient-centered care, my background in massage therapy and wellness education has shaped a deep understanding of how patients seek information, build trust, and make decisions about their care.

Today, I partner with women’s health and reproductive care practices to translate complex expertise into clear, compassionate communication—from websites and patient education resources to ongoing content that helps patients feel informed and supported from their very first interaction.

My connection to this work became deeply personal through my own experiences with pregnancy, birth, and motherhood, deepening my appreciation for the wisdom and compassion women’s health professionals bring to families during some of life’s most significant moments.

I believe the best healthcare communication doesn’t try to manufacture trust. It makes the care, expertise, and compassion already there easier for patients to recognize.

My Philosophy

Copy doesn't create trust. It reveals the trust you've already earned.

I believe that if marketing improves your effectiveness but sacrifices respect, honesty, or integrity along the way, it's not success—it's wasted work.

When we work together, my goal isn't to make you feel like you've fallen short. It's to help you recognize the strengths you've already built and bring them to the surface.

I see every project as a partnership. I listen first, ask thoughtful questions, and create something that feels true to your practice, your values, and the people you serve.

At the end of the day, I want to close my computer knowing I've helped someone who spends their days helping others. That's the kind of business I want to build, and the kind of work I'm proud to put my name on.
