ABOUT

Hi, I'm Katie.

I was raised in Hilton Head, South Carolina, and after spending several years away during my husband's Army service, our family returned to the Lowcountry to put down roots again.

For more than thirteen years, I've worked as a licensed massage therapist. In 2016, I earned my RYT certification and spent time teaching yoga. Today, I'm a wife, a mother of three (with our fourth on the way!), and I'm pursuing a nursing degree while growing my copywriting business.

I've always loved writing, especially untangling complicated thoughts and ideas until they become clear, approachable, and genuinely helpful. That passion led me to earn my copywriting certification in 2022, and it's something I continue to bring to every project.

Serving others has been a common thread throughout my life, whether through massage therapy, yoga, writing, or now pursuing nursing. But my passion for serving birth and women's health professionals became deeply personal when I had my own children.

The births of my children were some of the most empowering experiences of my life. They gave me an even greater appreciation for the wisdom, calm presence, and compassionate care that birth and women's health professionals bring to families. That's one of the reasons I feel so honored to support the people who make those moments possible for others.

If our values align, I'd love to help make sure your words reflect the same care, professionalism, and heart your clients experience every day.

My Philosophy

Copy doesn't create trust. It reveals the trust you've already earned.

I believe that if marketing improves your effectiveness but sacrifices respect, honesty, or integrity along the way, it's not success—it's wasted work.

When we work together, my goal isn't to make you feel like you've fallen short. It's to help you recognize the strengths you've already built and bring them to the surface.

I see every project as a partnership. I listen first, ask thoughtful questions, and create something that feels true to your practice, your values, and the people you serve.

At the end of the day, I want to close my computer knowing I've helped someone who spends their days helping others. That's the kind of business I want to build, and the kind of work I'm proud to put my name on.

A GOOD SIGN IT'S TIME

When is it time to bring in a copywriter?

Sometimes the need is obvious. More often, it quietly builds over time.

You're launching a new service. Your website no longer reflects your practice. You have ideas but no time to write them. Your messaging feels inconsistent. Important updates keep getting pushed to "later." You know what you want to say—you just don't have the time or words to say it well.

You don't have to wait until everything feels outdated. Sometimes having someone in your corner to help present your expertise with confidence is enough to keep your business moving forward.
